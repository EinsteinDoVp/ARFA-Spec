# Support ARFA Research

ARFA (Adaptive Relational Field Architecture) is an independent open-source
research project exploring closed-loop epistemic containment and dynamic
autonomy modulation for AI systems operating under changing conditions.

## What sponsorship supports
Funding is intended to support:
- implementation and testing of the public research prototype;
- reproducible synthetic experiments and ablation studies;
- technical documentation and open research artifacts;
- preparation of reproducibility packages and preprint materials.

## Current evidence
The repository currently includes an executable prototype, deterministic
synthetic environment, unit tests, GitHub Actions CI, and a minimal ablation
experiment.

## Important limitation
Sponsorship supports research and development. It does not imply that ARFA is
scientifically validated, production-ready, endorsed by GitHub, or proven to
improve real-world AI safety.

## Near-term deliverables
1. Prototype 0.3 with closer metric alignment to the specification.
2. Parameter sweeps and machine-readable experimental outputs.
3. Expanded ablations and documented threats to validity.
4. Versioned reproducibility release.
